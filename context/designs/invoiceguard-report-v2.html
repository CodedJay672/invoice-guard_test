What this means

This company has signed up to the Fair Payment Code, a voluntary scheme administered by the Small Business Commissioner. Gold Award signatories have committed to paying 95% of invoices within 60 days, with a standard payment term of 30 days or fewer. Signing the code is a public commitment to prompt payment practices — it does not guarantee payment but indicates the company has formally acknowledged its obligations to smaller suppliers.

Fair Payment Code Tiers Explained
